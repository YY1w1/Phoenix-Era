#### Script Summary:

- ###### 2000 BC~1000 BC　Development
> Human evolution, learning to use forging, agriculture and other most basic fundamental technologies

Phoenix Era:The Kindled Flame

- ###### 1000 BC~750 AD　Pre-antiquity
> An ancient society that has not invented gunpowder and so on, and has very little understanding of ship technology (basically active inland), but will develop many natural engineering projects.

Phoenix Era:Silent Incubation

- ###### 750 AD~1800 AD　Post-ancient
> Start researching gunpowder use, weapon diversification, and the Great Navigation Plan

Phoenix Era:Accidental Ignition

- ###### 1800 AD~2500 AD　World War
> Cession of territory > World War I > World War II > World War III
